# GTA Tumkur

A standalone, story-driven 3D campus adventure set at Siddaganga Institute of Technology (SIT), Tumakuru. Explore campus landmarks, meet Nila and Dev, make choices, and find your place in the welcome circle.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No installation, build step, internet connection, or external game engine is required.

## Controls

- **W A S D** or **arrow keys** — move
- **Shift** — run
- **Drag** the scene — look around
- **E** — interact or continue a conversation
- **1 / 2** — select a story choice
- **J** — open or close your journal
- **Esc** — close a conversation or journal
- Touchscreen users can move with the on-screen controls and drag to look around.

## Story

Start at the SIT main gate, meet Nila at the central quad, find her welcome-week poster on the campus notice board, visit the Student Activity Centre, and choose how to welcome the new students. Your choices shape your courage and connection scores and the ending.

The map uses SIT campus landmarks—including the main academic block, central library, canteen, hostels, auditorium, and sports ground. It is a stylized, walkable campus schematic, not a surveyed or turn-by-turn map; building placement and paths are approximate.
